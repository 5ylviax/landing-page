Signal Support Systems Specialist

I am a military veteran who served six years in the National Guard and two years in the Reserve as a Signal Support Systems Specialist, earning the rank of Sergeant (E-5).

My military experience helped me develop strong leadership, communication, critical thinking, adaptability, and problem-solving skills, which I now bring to software engineering and collaborative development environments.
